# Share a saved session

Use **Share** to upload a snapshot of a conversation to Radius with **Private** visibility. The upload includes the full saved session history. It includes saved branches and tool results, which may contain file paths and secrets. Review the history before uploading. Messages still being written are left out.

1. Open the session menu or its sidebar row menu and choose **Share**. You can also find **Share** in the command palette.
2. In **Share to Radius**, choose **Share** to confirm. You can continue working while the upload finishes.
3. When **Session shared** appears, choose **Copy link** or **Open link**. Save the link before dismissing the notification or reloading Piano.

Each confirmed share creates a separate artifact. Later messages stay in Piano until you share again. To publish a summary you can revise at the same link, [publish a document to Radius](../radius/radius-artifacts.md).

## If a share doesn’t finish

- **Cannot share** offers a **Providers** button. Open it, sign in to Radius, then share again. [Set up Radius](../radius/README.md)
- If there’s no saved history yet, wait for the session to save. Saved sessions over **25 MiB** are too large to share.
- If Piano can’t confirm whether an upload finished, check your artifacts in Radius before sharing again to avoid creating a second copy.
