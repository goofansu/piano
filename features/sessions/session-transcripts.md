# Read and navigate a transcript

Open a session from the sidebar to read your messages, Pi’s replies, and the tools Pi used. In a long conversation, use **Jump to message** to return to an earlier exchange. To focus on the conversation, open **Session information and actions** and turn on **Messages only**.

## Reuse part of a reply

- Choose **Copy response** to copy the reply’s original Markdown, including its line breaks, lists, and code blocks.
- With a mouse, select a passage in the transcript and choose **Add to chat**. The quote appears beside your draft so you can ask a follow-up question about that passage.

The transcript can contain sensitive text exactly as you or Pi entered it, including credentials in messages and tool output. Review it before you copy a reply or share the session; see [Share a saved session](../sharing/README.md).
